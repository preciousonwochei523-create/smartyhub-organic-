Delivery & Refund Policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

Delivery

  • We deliver within Lagos. Areas, fees and hours are under Delivery & hours in the menu.
  • Food is prepared fresh after you order, so delivery time depends on how busy we are and where you are. We will keep you updated.
  • Please give a correct address and a phone number that is reachable on delivery.
  • Food and supplements arrive separately. They are packed at different locations, so an order with both is delivered in two trips, at different times, each with its own delivery fee. You will get a separate order number for each.

Changing or cancelling an order

Message us on WhatsApp as soon as you can. Once we have started preparing food we may not be able to cancel or change it.

Problems with your order

If an item is missing, wrong, damaged or not as expected, message us on WhatsApp on the day of delivery with your order number and a photo. We will replace the item or refund you, depending on the situation.

Supplements

For wrong or damaged supplements, contact us within 48 hours of delivery. For safety and hygiene, we cannot take back supplements that have been opened.

Refunds

Approved refunds are sent back using the same method you paid with, or as agreed with you on WhatsApp.

Promo codes and free vitamin sachet

Promo codes follow the conditions shown with each offer. The free vitamin sachet comes with every food order while stocks last.
