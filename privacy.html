Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, phone number, delivery address, the items you order and any note you add.
  • Account details: if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture.
  • Your activity: your order history, promo codes used and the ratings you give.

How we use it

  • To prepare, deliver and track your orders.
  • To show your orders and member offers when you sign in.
  • To contact you about an order, for example on WhatsApp or by phone.
  • To show average ratings on our menu and to improve our service.

Who sees it

We do not sell your information. It is shared only with the people and services needed to run the shop: our delivery team, our website and database provider, Google (for sign-in) and WhatsApp when you message us. Ratings are shown on the menu as averages only, without your name.

Keeping it safe

Your data is stored with a secure hosting provider and access is limited. Signed-in customers can only see their own orders.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your information, or close your account, by messaging us on WhatsApp. We handle your data in line with applicable Nigerian data protection law.

Changes

We may update this page. The date at the top shows the latest version.
